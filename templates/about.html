Security notice

All events are simulated locally. No real attacks are performed. No external systems are contacted by the application. No operating-system containment actions are performed.

This is a single-user local teaching tool, not a real SIEM, endpoint security product, or production incident management service.

Do not expose the Flask development server to the internet or store real incident data.
