# Educational NIST mapping

This mapping is interpretive, not a certification or compliance assessment.

NIST CSF 2.0 function → lab illustration:

- Govern: documented roles, escalation ownership and communication decisions.
- Identify: fictional host/user context, scope assessment and lessons learned.
- Protect: preparation discussions and proposed control improvements; no preventive controls are deployed.
- Detect: synthetic collection, normalization, correlation and analyst triage.
- Respond: investigation, prioritization and recorded containment/eradication decisions.
- Recover: simulated restoration planning and recovery validation before closure.

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 integrates incident response into CSF 2.0 cybersecurity risk management. Detect, Respond and Recover describe incident response activities; Govern, Identify and Protect support readiness. Improvement feeds learning back into broader risk management.

OPEN through CLOSED is this application's simplified playbook, not a verbatim NIST lifecycle. Preparation and lessons learned are teaching practices within that broader framing. Real implementation requires organization-specific risk management, governance and capabilities beyond this application.
